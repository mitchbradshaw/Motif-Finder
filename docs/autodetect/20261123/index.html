How a candidate's confidence is built. R4 (the classifier trained on your verdicts) scores every candidate's own span. Each other source that agrees closes part of the remaining gap to certainty: confidence = 1 − (1 − R4) × Π (1 − ½ × precision of each agreeing source). Precisions: drop v1 0.955, drop v5 0.812, wavelet 0.862, BEADS 0.675 (thesis, M2_aug), seed matches 0.9, BEADS σ 0.75 (provisional). A candidate R4's window scan found alone keeps R4's score. Found means a candidate covers at least half of the shorter of it and your accepted span; hit rate counts only candidates that land on reviewed material.
