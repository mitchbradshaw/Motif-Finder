I thank my primary supervisor, Prof Janet Wiles, for directing this project away from an early emphasis on building analysis machinery and toward the questions that the machinery is meant to answer, and in particular for pushing me to examine the signals from different perspectives. I thank Dr Matthew D'Souza and Dr Karl Bertling for their guidance on the recording apparatus and on the electrical characteristics of the signals being measured, and for their advice on distinguishing artefacts of the recording configuration from behaviour of the specimen.

I thank the wider Underground Brains group at UQ for access to the recordings analysed here, and for the growing, wiring and maintenance of the cultures from which they were taken. The datasets used in this thesis are the product of a considerable amount of other people's laboratory work.
