\chapter{Drop-motif claims ledger}
\label{app:claims}

The drop-motif study kept a ledger of every claim it tested, with the evidence, the null that bounds it and a verdict. Each falsified or bounded claim carries the sentence to use instead. Totals: 7 supported, 3 bounded, 3 not supported and 7 falsified. The full ledger is \texttt{Plots/drop\_motifs10/CLAIMS.md}.
