# What the enlarged Library changes in Chapter 10 — 8 November 2026

The questions remain useful. Their populations and counting units now need more care. More accepted examples
broaden coverage, while timing/recovery need ordered individual events from defined recording spans. A train
must retain its separate members and a parent sequence, and cannot be substituted for one large waveform.

| Question | Effect of the new material | Current answer and next requirement |
|---|---|---|

The gap-order result disappears at 2 mV. Historical full-channel results retain their own cohort and correction. A sampled Library is not a recording-wide timing census. |

The new 850-item queue prioritises the most useful extra labels now, rather than waiting for CNN completion.
