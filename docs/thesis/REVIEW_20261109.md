### Voice

Removed or shortened the stacked caveats that read as machine-written ("rather than…", "does not establish…",
"These are X, not Y" closing nearly every paragraph), process dates ("returned on 8 November"), and software
internals (verdict resolvers, sequence containers, live queue counters). Caveats that matter now appear once, where
they bite, and the chapter discussions keep the limitations. Heavy statistics in Ch6 (binomial tails of 10⁻²²,
exchangeable nulls) were replaced by a plain-language reading with the formal test left in the table; the pooled
null-AUC explanation in Ch8 and the κ note in Ch7 became footnotes.
