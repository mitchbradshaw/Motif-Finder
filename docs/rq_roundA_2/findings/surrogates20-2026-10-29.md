# Six precise variants against a 20-draw null (2026-10-29)

This is the RQ4b read-out's step 3: each chosen variant against a 20-draw null. It closes the question
`detectors-vs-surrogates-2026-10-26.md` left open.

**Code:** `scripts/thesis/surrogates20_readout.py`, test `tests/test_surrogates20_readout.py`.

**Inputs:** the cluster's returned task records, `docs/thesis/hpc/surrogates20_M2_aug/*_out/return/task*/result.json`.
That is 96 tasks, all complete. Each holds the real run's count and each of its 20 draws' counts.

**Outputs:** `docs/thesis/data/ch06_20draw/`: `per_channel.csv`, `per_variant.csv`, `summary.json` and
`ratio_strip.png`.

Nothing was read from or written to the database. The runs are not imported yet.

## In plain words

A detector that finds something real in a recording should fire more on the recording than on copies of it whose
frequency content is kept but whose timing is scrambled (phase-randomised surrogates). On 26 Oct each channel had
one such copy: a single coin toss. Now each of 16 M2_aug channels has 20 copies, so each channel can be ranked: did
the real count beat all 20? By chance that happens to about one channel in 21, or 0.8 of 16.

- **Four variants beat their null on nearly every channel:** drop v1, the GADF and recurrence CNNs, and v5 at
  same-fraction 0.7.
- **Two fire *more* on the scrambled copies on nearly every channel:** wavelet-encoded level 5 and v1 at slope σ 4.
- **The single draw was not noise.** Every pooled ratio is within 4 % of its 1-draw value, so how a detector stands
  against its null is a stable property of that detector.
- **The two "below the null" variants are among the most precise.** They score 0.92 and 0.99 in the blind estimate.
  Beating a phase-randomised null and being precise are different things, as Chapter 6 argued from one draw; it
  now rests on 20.

## Protocol

- **Data:** M2_aug, all 16 channels, the whole 721 h.
- **Null:** phase randomisation, seed 0, 20 draws per channel.
- **Variants:** the six most precise of four families, per-setting precision from queue 25 (spec hashes in the
  bundle's `manifest.json`).
- **Run:** on rangpur through the overnight sweep's machinery: four packs, 96 tasks of 21 chain runs each.
- **Per channel:**
  - p_high = (1 + draws ≥ real) / 21, for real above its null; p_low = (1 + draws ≤ real) / 21, for real below. The
    floor is 1/21 ≈ 0.048.
  - The ratio is real / mean(draws), and z = (real − mean) / sd.
- **Per variant:**
  - The number of channels whose real count lies above every draw ("beat all") or below every draw ("below all"),
    each tested against Binomial(16, 1/21).
  - Benjamini–Hochberg across the six variants, on the pre-specified direction (real above its null).
  - The pooled count over all channels, against the 20 pooled draws (the i-th draw summed across channels).

## Reading it

- **Stable, not noisy.** The 1-draw and 20-draw ratios agree to within 4 % for every variant, from 0.075 to 23. The
  surrogate count barely moves between draws, so the 26 Oct table (`tab:detnull`) stands as measured. What it
  measures is a property of each detector.
- **Two kinds of precise detector.**
  - Drop v1 and the 10-min CNNs fire on structure that phase randomisation destroys: the timing of the signal's
    frequencies, not only their power.
  - Wavelet-encoded level 5 and v1 at slope σ 4 fire more on a scrambled copy.
  - A likely reason, not tested here: a detector with a relative threshold fires on any fluctuation of its scale.
    Scrambling spreads the recording's large slow excursions into many mid-sized ones.
  - Both kinds are precise when a human judges their events. So a detector's standing against a phase-randomised
    null is not evidence about the quality of its events, either way.
- **For the thesis (Chapter 6):** judge detectors by precision against blind human verdicts, as the chapter does. Use
  the null as a description of what a detector responds to, not as a quality score.

## Caveats

1. **The binomial tails treat the 16 channels as independent.** The B4 census found a shared driver across packs
   (Chapter 10), so the tails are approximate. The per-channel ranks are exact.
2. **The p floor is 1/21.** Twenty draws cannot show "beyond chance" on a single channel at α 0.05 with a correction.
   The per-variant counts are the test.
3. **One recording (M2_aug), six variants, one null method.** No other null was tried, such as block shuffles or AAFT.
4. **The runs are not in the database yet.** Importing them, through 8765 into Discovery session 2, is a separate step
   (`TASKS.md` §3c). The numbers here do not depend on it.
