# Miss rate: the old 10-minute "interesting" windows that no detector touches, 2026-10-26

Made by `scripts/thesis/miss_rate.py` (read-only). Outputs: `docs/thesis/data/miss_rate/per_variant.csv` and
`summary.json`.

## In plain words

Before the tool existed, the researcher marked 10-minute windows of M2_aug as interesting or not. If the detectors
are any good, they should fire inside nearly every interesting window, and fire less often inside the dull ones.
Think of 46 smoke alarms in a building: the question is how many real fires none of them noticed, and for each alarm,
how much more often it goes off in a room with a fire than in one without.

**Almost nothing is missed, but mostly because the loose detectors fire almost everywhere.** Only 18 of the 2,333
interesting windows (0.8 %) are touched by none of the 34 non-CNN detector settings. But those same settings also
touch 95 % of the not-interesting windows. The useful number is the lift: the selective detectors are rare but
pointed (they touch an interesting window 5–21 times as often as a dull one); the loose ones touch both.

## The labels

`annotations` with `source = 'imported_10min'` on M2_aug_concat_fs1 (written 2026-08-03 by the importer of the
2025–26 manual labels): **2,333 interesting, 8,773 not_interesting, 128 artifact**, each exactly 600 samples
(10 min at 1 Hz). Artifact windows are left out.

## The detectors

Every live template run of Discovery session 2 (M2_aug), one run group per variant: **46 variants** (the 13 base
templates' sweep runs and their variants, the 12 CNN templates, the matrix-profile copies). Real runs only. A
detection *touches* a window if they overlap at all. Four base templates (drop_detection_v5, symbol_search,
Wavelet_detection_prototype, deconvolved_troughs) ran on CH1_A1 and CH2_A1 only and are scored there (272
interesting, 1,058 not); the matrix-profile copies ran on those two channels too.

**The CNN templates are in-sample:** the four models were very likely trained on these same windows (W4-A). Their
rows are shown, but not used for the headline.

## What it means

- **A miss rate near zero is cheap**: OASIS at threshold 3 alone touches 91 % of interesting windows, but also 66 %
  of the dull ones. Any union that includes the loose settings misses almost nothing and selects almost nothing.
- **The pointed detectors are rare**: the three most precise families (drop_detection_v1 / v5, wavelet_encoded) touch
  only 14 % of the interesting windows between them. Most of what the researcher marked interesting in 2025–26 is not
  a single sharp drop of the kind they find.
- **signal_threshold at −10 mV has the highest lift** (21) at a 12 % touch rate: a plain amplitude cut tracks the
  old labels well, which says the old labels lean on size.
- **mp_threshold at 6 is anti-correlated** with the labels (lift 0.53): it fires in the quiet windows.
- The 18 untouched interesting windows (annotation ids, recording and span) are listed in `summary.json`
  (`untouched_interesting_no_cnn`): the re-look list for "what kind of interesting no detector sees".
