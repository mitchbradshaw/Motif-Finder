% Appendix entry: detection.freq_stft -- "STFT spectrum" (Appendix A).
\entryfield{Algorithm}
\code{_run} in \code{Adapters/detection_freq_stft.py} calls \code{stft_log_spectrum} in
\code{Working/Detection/analysis/freq_analysis.py} with \code{return_2d=False}. With window
length $w$, hop $h$ and $B$ log bins:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Refuse a span shorter than $w$ (\code{ValueError}).
\item Short-time Fourier transform with \code{scipy.signal.stft}
  \citep{virtanen2020scipy}: Hann window of $w$ samples, overlap $w-h$, no boundary
  padding, no padding of the last frame, no detrending, SciPy's default scaling and
  one-sided spectrum. The FFT frequencies are $f_k = k f_s/w$, $k = 0,\dots,\lfloor w/2\rfloor$.
\item Power $P_{k,t} = |Z_{k,t}|^2$ for each frequency $k$ and frame $t$.
\item Bin edges $e_0,\dots,e_B$ spaced logarithmically from the lowest non-zero frequency
  $f_1 = f_s/w$ to the highest, $f_{\lfloor w/2\rfloor}$ (Nyquist for even $w$). The
  0\,Hz bin is excluded. Bin centres are geometric: $\sqrt{e_b e_{b+1}}$.
\item For each log bin $b$, average $P_{k,t}$ over the FFT frequencies with
  $e_b \leq f_k < e_{b+1}$; a bin that holds none is left at 0.
\item Average over frames: the output is $\bar P_b = \frac{1}{T}\sum_t P_{b,t}$.
\end{enumerate}

\entryfield{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Units.} Not unit-aware: power is in the stored unit squared (scaled by SciPy's
  window normalisation), so spectra of a volts recording and a millivolts one differ by
  $10^6$.
\item \emph{Sampling rate.} \emph{window\_size} and \emph{hop\_size} are in samples. The
  lowest frequency resolved is $f_s/w$: at the defaults, 1/300\,Hz (a 5-minute period) at
  1\,Hz sampling and 1/30\,Hz at 10\,Hz.
\item \emph{Baseline.} No detrending: a large standing potential leaks through the Hann
  window's main lobe into the lowest bins. Remove the baseline first when the slow end
  matters.
\item \emph{Missing values.} No guard. A NaN makes its frames' power NaN at every frequency,
  and the plain mean over frames then makes every non-empty bin NaN.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Outputs}
An \code{Encoding} of kind \code{image} holding a one-dimensional array of $B$ mean powers
(not a spectrogram: the time axis is averaged away), with the bin centres in
\code{meta["bin_freqs"]} in hertz. The bridge sends it as a series with its frequencies,
summarised as ``$B$ log-frequency bins''. The full frame-by-bin matrix
(\code{return_2d=True}) is not reachable from the block.

\entryfield{Cost}
$\lceil (n-w)/h\rceil$ FFTs of length $w$: $O\big((n/h)\, w\log w\big)$ time. The whole
complex STFT matrix and its power are held at once: about $(n/h)(w/2+1)\cdot 24$ bytes,
some 60\,MB for a 2.6-million-sample span at the defaults. No \code{estimate} hook.

\entryfield{Failure modes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Empty low bins read as zero.} The log bins are narrower than the FFT spacing
  $f_s/w$ at the slow end. At the defaults ($w = 300$, $B = 64$) the FFT frequencies $k=1$
  to 17 fall in only 17 of the lowest 37 bins, so \unverified{about 20 of the 64 bins} are
  exactly 0, independently of the data and of $f_s$. Plot it on a log axis and they vanish;
  read it as a vector (by a later block) and they are false zeros.
\item \emph{The top FFT frequency may be dropped.} The last edge equals the highest FFT
  frequency up to rounding, and the upper test is strict, so that frequency is
  \unverified{usually left out of the last bin}.
\item \emph{A short span.} Fewer than $w$ samples raise an error; just over $w$ gives one
  frame and a noisy spectrum.
\item \emph{hop\_size larger than window\_size} skips samples between windows;
  \unverified{SciPy accepts the resulting negative overlap}.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Validation}
\code{tests/test_analysis_modules.py} checks shapes (64 bins, other bin counts), that the
1-D output is the mean of the 2-D frames, that bin centres are positive, increasing and below
Nyquist, that power is non-negative and that a short signal is refused. It runs a 5\,Hz
sine but does not check that the power peaks in the bin containing 5\,Hz. No test checks
the empty bins.
