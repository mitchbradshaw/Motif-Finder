% Appendix entry: detection.pan_matrix_profile -- "Pan matrix profile" (Appendix A).
\entryfield{Algorithm}
The pan matrix profile of \citet{madrid2019matrix} (matrix profile XX) stacks matrix profiles over a
range of window lengths. \code{_run} in \code{Adapters/detection_pan_matrix_profile.py} calls
\code{pan_matrix_profile} in \code{Working/Detection/matrix_profiling/pan.py}, whose docstring sets
out every rule; in order:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Ladder} (\code{window_lengths}). \emph{n\_lengths} lengths spaced geometrically from
  \emph{min\_window\_s} to \emph{max\_window\_s}, $m = \mathrm{round}(s f_s)$, duplicates removed. A
  length below 4 samples or above half the span is dropped and named. One exact
  \code{stumpy.stump} per length, not stumpy's \code{stimp}, which visits a linear ladder and whose
  \code{PAN_} is binarised at 0.2 by default.
\item \emph{Gaps and stalls} (\code{prepare_gaps}). NaN runs of at most \emph{max\_fill\_s} are
  bridged by a straight line; longer runs stay NaN. A stall, a run of at least \emph{stall\_s} where
  every second difference $|x_{i+2} - 2x_{i+1} + x_i|$ is within 1\,\% of the span's 75th percentile
  of them (\code{stall_mask}, \code{STALL_TOL}), is made NaN too, because a held value, detrended,
  is a ramp that matches every other ramp exactly. Both are counted.
\item \emph{Brightness} (\code{normalised_profile}). For each length, the exact z-normalised
  matrix profile (\blockentry{detection.matrix_profile}; exclusion zone $m/4$), scaled as stumpy
  scales its pan profile, $\min\bigl(d/(2\sqrt m),\,1\bigr)$, so that every length shares one 0--1
  scale (the paper gives no per-length rule); the image holds $1 -$ that. 1 means an identical shape
  exists elsewhere; about 0.29 is what two windows of white noise score; 0, anti-correlated. A
  window touching a long gap or stall is NaN.
\item \emph{Distinct windows} (\code{distinct_values}). Sort the profile and keep a window only if no
  kept window starts within $m$ samples: $N$ non-overlapping windows. $r_K$ is the mean of the best
  $K = 3$, so one lucky pair cannot carry a length.
\item \emph{Null.} For each length that can be judged (step 6), \emph{null\_draws} copies of the
  filled span are profiled the same way: \texttt{aaft} (the default) is the amplitude-adjusted
  Fourier transform surrogate of \citet{theiler1992testing} (the recording's own values, reordered
  so the spectrum is roughly kept and every shape destroyed;
  \code{aaft_surrogate} in \code{Adapters/preprocessing_surrogate.py}); \texttt{phase} keeps the
  spectrum exactly but makes the values bell-shaped, and is kept for comparison only. Each draw is
  seeded by (\emph{seed}, draw number), so every length sees the same copies, and the long gaps are
  put back. From each draw's distinct windows: $z_K$ (mean of the best 3) and $z_{\mathrm{med}}$
  (the median), averaged over draws.
\item \emph{Verdict} (\code{length_verdict}). A length is judged only if the usable samples make at
  least 14 windows (\code{MIN_WINDOWS}); then
  \[ \mathrm{gap} = N^{1/3}\,\frac{\ln(z_K / r_K)}{\ln(z_{\mathrm{med}} / z_K)}, \]
  how far the recording's best look-alikes reach beyond noise's best, in units of how far noise's
  best reach beyond its typical window. The length \emph{clears} when the gap reaches the cut,
  \code{GAP_MIN} $= 6$ once the span holds 24 windows, rising linearly to about 8.5 at 14
  (\code{gap_cut}), \emph{and} $r_K$ is below every single draw's $z_K$. The exponent $1/3$, the
  cut and the window counts are measured on synthetic noise (calibration tables beside the
  constants in \code{pan.py}), not derived.
\item \emph{Answer.} The band of cleared lengths (\code{cleared_s}) and the cleared length with the
  largest gap (\code{suggested_window_s}; the summary says ``strongest gap at''). If none clears:
  ``no length stands out''; the shortest length is never a fallback.
\end{enumerate}

\entryfield{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Units.} Z-normalised throughout, so units do not matter. A constant span is reported as
  ``no spread to measure''.
\item \emph{Sampling rate.} Lengths are in seconds, converted at $f_s$; a short length at a low rate
  has few samples (30\,s at 0.5\,Hz is 15), and z-normalising so few samples is itself noisy, which
  the measured exponent allows for.
\item \emph{Missing values.} Short NaN runs are bridged by a line, long ones and stalls left out of
  the picture and every statistic; real and null lose the same windows. The default
  \emph{max\_fill\_s} (5\,s) and \emph{stall\_s} (60\,s) are marked as placeholders.
\item \emph{Detrending.} Assumed done upstream: the gap was calibrated after a 2\,h rolling-mean
  detrend, and slow drift otherwise dominates long windows.
\item \emph{Same length.} Two occurrences of a shape at different durations do not match at any
  single length.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Outputs}
An \texttt{Encoding} of kind \texttt{image}: a float32 array with one row per length, longest first
(row 0 is the longest), and one column per sample of the span, start-aligned: column $i$ is the
window starting at sample $i$, so each row's last $m - 1$ columns are NaN. Followed by
\blockref{detection.wavelet_summation}, a band of rows sums to ``how strongly the windows starting
here repeat across these lengths''. \code{meta} carries \code{lengths_s} and
\code{lengths_samples} (row order), \code{lengths_dropped_samples}, \code{per_length} (per row: the
best distance and where, 5th percentile, median, window counts, $N$, $r_K$, the null's $z_K$ per
draw and median, gap, cut, judged, clears, and the reason in words), \code{cleared_s},
\code{suggested_window_s}, \code{suggested_row}, \code{verdict}, \code{reasons},
\code{n_filled}, \code{n_long_gap}, \code{n_stalled}, the null settings, \code{alignment}
(\texttt{start}), \code{summary} and \code{rules}. Nothing is persisted beyond the run's own
result.

\entryfield{Cost}
One exact matrix profile ($O(n^2)$, flat in $m$) per length, plus \emph{null\_draws} per judged
length: at the defaults up to $12 + 3\times 12 = 48$ profiles of the whole span. The estimate is the
matrix-profile cost model times that count (\code{profile_units}), plus about 40\,s for stumpy's
first compilation in a fresh process (\code{FIRST_CALL_S}). There is no approximate mode: stumpy's
\code{scrump} drew from numpy's global random generator and was not reproducible beside other
threads. Memory is the limit that is enforced: about 1\,kB per sample at peak (16 numba threads),
so the span is capped at $10^6$ samples (\code{MAX_SPAN_SAMPLES}, about 277\,h at 1\,Hz). Progress
is reported after every profile and a cancel is honoured before each. Over the Analyse ceiling a
chain is sent to the cluster.

\entryfield{Failure modes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Reading the strongest gap as an event length.} It is not: it leans towards the longest
  judged length when an event dominates long windows (a planted 90\,s spike gave anything from 30 to
  976\,s) and towards an edge on drop chains (the 30--72\,s fall); in a later check it fell near
  either planted motif in 2 of 10 seeds. The band holds the event length; the strongest gap does
  not pick it out.
\item \emph{Lengths out of reach.} A length is judged only on a span 14 windows long: an 8\,h span
  cannot judge a drop chain's roughly 30-minute unit (expect ``no length'' or the fall edge); judge
  chains on 24\,h or more.
\item \emph{A null that keeps the rhythm.} The null keeps the spectrum, so a quasi-periodic chain
  keeps its spacing in its own copies and its period is partly in the null.
\item \emph{Twice-only motifs.} A shape that occurs only twice barely moves $r_K$ ($K = 3$).
\item \emph{Amplitude is invisible.} A quiet stretch and a loud one of the same shape match; pure
  noise has no shape of its own.
\item \emph{On real recordings everything repeats.} Every judged length repeated beyond the null on
  the real chains and the spiking recording tried
  (\code{docs/prompts/blocks/reports/W3-evaluation.md}, as summarised in
  \code{docs/research/analysis-blocks-primary-sources.md}, 13b), so a cleared band there says less
  than on synthetic data.
\end{itemize}
Window versus event: the picture is start-aligned and says so (\code{meta["alignment"]}); a bright
pixel marks where a repeating window \emph{starts}, not where the event is.

\entryfield{Validation}
The false-alarm rate and the cut were measured, not derived: at the defaults no suggestion on 150
spans with no structure (random walks, AR(1), pink noise, a lopsided exp-AR(1) series), and 6 of 6
suggestions on spans with planted motifs, 5 within one rung of a planted scale (table beside
\code{GAP_MIN} in \code{pan.py}; measured with a scratch script the comment names, \code{w3c_hold/fix1_sweep.py}).
\code{tests/test_pan_matrix_profile.py} checks the answer on synthetic recordings
(\code{tests/_pan_synthetic.py}): two planted motif lengths both clear the null and are bright where
they sit; spans with no structure (2\,h and 4\,h, and a lopsided series) rarely or never suggest a
length; a dominant motif clears a band holding its length; the shortest length is never a
fallback; and flatlines, quantised loggers and stalls are handled as described. It also checks the
layout (rows long first, start-aligned, NaN tail, distances not the binarised pan), the ladder, the
gaps, and progress and cancelling. \code{tests/test_adapter_pan_matrix_profile.py} checks the
meta, the summary's caveat, the estimate (the cost model times the profile count, plus the first
compile), the memory guard, the recommended \emph{max\_window\_s}, and that
\blockref{detection.wavelet_summation} after it sums the intended lengths. No test uses a real
recording.
