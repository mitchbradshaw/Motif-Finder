\entryfield{Algorithm}
Time-series snippets are matrix profile XIII \citep{imani2018matrix}. \code{_run} in
\code{Adapters/catalogue_snippets.py} subtracts the span's offset from the \texttt{WindowSet}'s
channel-absolute starts and calls \code{find_snippets} in
\code{Working/Detection/matrix_profiling/snippets.py}. With $m$ the window length:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Pieces.} If the windows are contiguous (each starts where the last ended), the analysed
  stretch runs from the first window's start to the last window's end; otherwise it is the whole
  span. The pieces are the non-overlapping stretches $S_i$ of length $m$ cut from its start
  ($W = \lfloor n/m \rfloor$ of them), so with contiguous windows the pieces \emph{are} the windows.
  $m$ must be at most half the stretch.
\item \emph{Missing values} (adaptation). NaN is filled by straight-line interpolation (ends held
  flat). A piece more than half NaN (\code{MAX_NAN_FRACTION}) is never a snippet, and positions
  centred in it are left out of every area and fraction. At least $2\,k_{\max}$ usable pieces are
  required.
\item \emph{MPdist profiles.} The distance is MPdist \citep{gharghabi2018matrix}, not the
  whole-piece Euclidean distance. With sub-length $s = \min(\lceil \textit{subseq\_frac}\cdot
  m\rceil, m)$ and $j = m - s + 1$ sub-pieces per piece: for a piece $S_i$ and a position $p$, take
  the z-normalised Euclidean distance between every sub-piece of $S_i$ and every sub-piece of
  $x_{p..p+m-1}$; collect each row's minimum and each column's minimum ($2j$ values); MPdist is
  the value at place $\kappa = \min(\lceil \textit{mpdist\_percentage}\cdot 2m\rceil, 2j - 1)$ in
  that list sorted from 0. Two pieces are close when enough of their sub-pieces have close matches
  somewhere in the other, wherever they sit. At \emph{subseq\_frac} = 1, $s = m$ and MPdist is
  the plain z-normalised distance, which is stumpy's default and what the paper's method is not.
  Doing this for every piece and every position gives the profiles $D_i(p)$. The rules are
  stumpy's; the kernel is this project's rewrite (\code{mpdist_profiles}), which streams the
  distances instead of holding them, and is checked equal to stumpy's profiles.
\item \emph{Greedy choice} (paper, Table I; \code{greedy_snippets}). $Q \gets \infty$; for
  $k = 1, 2, \dots, k_{\max}+1$ pick the usable piece minimising $\sum_p \min(D_i(p), Q(p))$, record
  that sum as $\mathrm{Area}_k$, and set $Q \gets \min(Q, D_i)$.
\item \emph{How many} (\code{knee_detail}; two adaptations). With
  $\mathrm{change}_k = \mathrm{Area}_{k-1}/\mathrm{Area}_k - 1$, the candidate is the $k$ in
  $2..k_{\max}$ with the largest drop $\mathrm{change}_k - \mathrm{change}_{k+1}$ (the paper takes
  the peak of $\mathrm{change}_k$). It is accepted only if $\mathrm{change}_k$ is at least
  $c = \max(3,\ 0.65\sqrt{W})$ times the median of the later changes; otherwise $k = 1$, which the
  paper has no way to say. Both constants are placeholders measured on synthetic noise. A \emph{k}
  above 0 overrides the knee.
\item \emph{Regimes and labels.} Each position $p$ belongs to the chosen snippet with the lowest
  $D_i(p)$ (ties to the earlier); a snippet's fraction is its share of the scored positions.
  Position $p$ stands for the stretch starting at $p$, so it votes for the window containing its
  \emph{centre} $p + \lfloor m/2\rfloor$; each window takes the label with most votes (ties to the
  lower label). A snippet's own window is always given its own label, and how many windows that
  overrode is counted. A window more than half NaN is $-1$.
\item \emph{Run lengths.} Runs of one label over consecutive windows ($-1$ ends a run); the mean
  run is compared with the mean expected if the same labels were shuffled,
  $n / \bigl(1 + (n-1)(1 - \sum_c p_c^2)\bigr)$; a ratio of 1 means no more persistent than chance.
\end{enumerate}

\entryfield{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Units.} Every distance is z-normalised per sub-piece, so units do not matter. The span
  is also standardised once by its own mean and spread before computing, which changes no distance
  and only protects precision on recordings stored in volts with a large offset.
\item \emph{Sampling rate.} The piece length is the windows' length in samples; \code{meta} gives it
  in seconds too.
\item \emph{Detrending.} MPdist tolerates a trend within a sub-piece, not a wandering level that
  changes what the sub-pieces look like; remove the baseline first.
\item \emph{Missing values.} Filled by a line for the arithmetic, then excluded as above; the
  counts are in \code{meta}.
\item \emph{Pieces and boundaries.} Pieces are cut at fixed multiples of $m$, so a regime change
  inside a piece splits that piece's vote; behaviour changing faster than one piece is averaged into
  whichever snippet it half-resembles.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Outputs}
A \texttt{Grouping}: one integer label per input window, 1 to $k$ or $-1$. \code{meta} holds
\code{k}, \code{k_chosen_by} (\texttt{knee} or \texttt{parameter}), \code{k_reason} in words, the knee
figures, \code{areas} and \code{change_k}, \code{snippet_starts} (span-relative samples) and
\code{snippet_starts_s}, \code{fractions}, \code{exemplar_windows} (each label's own window),
\code{n_exemplar_relabelled}, \code{contiguous}, \code{m}, \code{s}, \code{kth}, piece, window, NaN
and position counts, \code{runs} and \code{runs_by_k} (run lengths at every fixed $k$, computed
without the exemplar override), \code{run_length_mean}, \code{run_length_median},
\code{run_length_vs_shuffled}, \code{elapsed_s}, \code{exemplar_rule}, \code{rules} and
\code{summary}.

\entryfield{Cost}
$O(n^2)$ work, nearly independent of $m$ ($n/m$ pieces against $n$ positions, $2j$ values each),
and $O(n^2/m)$ memory for the profiles. Measured here: 24\,h at 1\,Hz with hour-long pieces in about
24\,s, 72\,h in 228\,s. The estimate comes from a per-machine cost model
(\code{register_cost_model} with exponent 2, \code{Working/block_cost.py}) and is empty until
calibrated. Two limits: \code{max_span_samples} $= 604\,800$ samples, a week at 1\,Hz; and
\code{find_snippets} refuses any run whose memory peak (\code{memory_peak}) would exceed 4\,GB,
about 3\,GB at 7 days with hour-long pieces.

\entryfield{Failure modes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Regimes no better than chance on real data.} On two 24\,h M2\_aug spans and one drop
  chain, the regimes were no more persistent than on phase-randomised surrogates; on a second chain
  (72\,h) $k = 3$ regimes beat every surrogate, but the knee chose $k = 1$
  (\code{docs/prompts/blocks/reports/W2-evaluation.md}, as the registry description reports). Set
  \emph{k} by hand and read the run lengths and their ratio to shuffled.
\item \emph{Noise has no shape.} After z-normalisation a quiet stretch of noise matches nothing in
  particular and joins whichever snippet it happens to resemble; a regime differing only in
  amplitude is not separated (a test records this as an expected limitation).
\item \emph{Too few pieces for the knee.} On noise the knee-to-tail ratio grows about as
  $\sqrt{W}$; the cut follows it, but the margin is thin at 120 pieces (an 8\,h regime measured 8.1
  against a cut of 7.1). Below 24 pieces the automatic \emph{k} is not to be trusted.
\item \emph{subseq\_frac = 1.} Turns MPdist into whole-piece distance; on drifting synthetic
  recordings this lost the regimes.
\item \emph{The week limit is in samples.} 604\,800 samples is a week only at 1\,Hz: at 10\,Hz the
  cap is under 17 hours, though the description says ``refused above 7 days''.
\end{itemize}
Neither artefact of the older report tool applies: pieces never overlap, and each position votes
for the window holding its centre rather than the one where its stretch starts, which corrects for
the start alignment of the profile.

\entryfield{Validation}
\code{tests/test_snippets.py} checks that the rewritten profiles equal stumpy's own and that the
greedy choice equals \code{stumpy.snippets}; that three planted regimes under drift are found and
that whole-piece distance does measurably worse; that synthetic noise gives $k = 1$ and short runs;
that a rare regime is still named; the knee rule against its tail; and the NaN, constant-signal and
refusal paths. \code{tests/test_template_recording_snippets.py} runs the template chain end to end on
three regimes and on noise. All are synthetic; the real-data evaluation is the report cited above.
