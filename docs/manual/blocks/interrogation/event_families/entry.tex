% Appendix entry: interrogation.event_families -- "Event families" (Appendix A).
\entryfield{Algorithm}
\code{_run} in \code{Adapters/interrogation_event_families.py} calls \code{event_families}
in \code{Working/Catalogue/shape_families.py}, which cuts windows with
\code{Working/Catalogue/event_windows.py} and groups them with the Library's engine,
\code{Working.library.grouping.engine.run_grouping} (unit \texttt{single\_motifs}, basis
\texttt{shape-distance}). A family found here and one found in the Library are the same
computation on the same vectors.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Cut a window per event} (\code{event_windows}, \code{cut}), on the chain's
  current signal $x$. The anchor is the span start (default), Event shape's
  \code{onset_idx}, or its \code{extremum_idx}, rounded to a sample; an event whose anchor
  column holds NaN falls back to its span start and is counted. The window has
  $L = \mathrm{round}(\textit{length\_s}\cdot f_s)$ samples ($\textit{length\_s} = 0$: twice
  the median of Event shape's \code{duration_s}, rounded up to 10\,s and clamped to
  60--7200\,s), and starts $\mathrm{round}(\textit{pre\_frac}\cdot L)$ samples before the
  anchor. A window starting before the span (\code{past_start}), ending after it
  (\code{past_end}) or with a NaN fraction above \emph{max\_nan\_frac}
  (\code{too_much_nan}) is left out; that event's reason is \code{no_window:<reason>}.
\item \emph{Screen} (\code{_screen}): a window with $\pm\infty$ is omitted; one with a few
  NaNs (at most \emph{max\_nan\_frac}) is filled by linear interpolation and counted. This is
  an adaptation: the Library's methods drop any row with a NaN.
\item \emph{Prepare and group.} The engine resamples every window to
  \emph{resample\_length} points (256) and z-normalises it, so amplitude and offset do not
  enter, only shape. Then the chosen method:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{hdbscan} \citep{campello2013density}: density-based crowds under the
    distance \emph{hdbscan\_distance} (SBD, the shape-based distance of
    \citet{paparrizos2015kshape}, by default); shapes outside every crowd are left out as
    \code{not_in_a_dense_family}. Above \emph{hdbscan\_sample} windows a seeded sample is
    fitted and the rest join the family of their nearest fitted member if they lie within
    its crowd.
  \item \texttt{kshape} \citep{paparrizos2015kshape}: $k$ families compared after sliding
    one shape along the other; $k = 0$ chooses $k$ from 2 to 12 by the best silhouette.
    Members further from their centre than 95\,\% of noise are left out as
    \code{past_cut} (at the default \emph{kshape\_omit\_d} = 0).
  \item \texttt{ward}: a hierarchical tree cut at \emph{ward\_cut}; every event placed
    unless further than \emph{ward\_omit\_d} from every family medoid. No noise test; refused
    above 5{,}000 windows (a square distance matrix).
  \end{itemize}
  The engine drops families under \emph{min\_group} members (\code{group_too_small}).
  The details of each method are the Library's (\code{Working/library/grouping/methods/}).
\item \emph{The noise test} (per family, by the method, read off the engine's flags).
  The null is the span's own quiet stretches: windows of length $L$ cut where no event span
  and no event window lies (\code{span_background}), used wherever at least half a family's
  members got one. Otherwise the null is trend-kept surrogates of the members (slow trend
  kept, the rest phase-randomised), and the family says so. Per the registry's settings, a
  \texttt{hdbscan} family passes when its members sit closer to its medoid than
  $0.9\times$ the 5th percentile of the trend-kept surrogates, or $0.6\times$ the
  selection-matched quantile of the quiet windows; a family that fails is
  \code{noise_like}. \unverified{These thresholds are taken from the registry's setting
  descriptions; the method code was not read line by line for this entry.}
\item \emph{Number} families $1..K$ by size, largest first (ties by earliest member). Each
  family's exemplar is its medoid, a member, never an average.
\item \emph{Coherence}: per family, \code{svd_entropy} (\code{Working/library/coherence.py})
  of its members aligned to the medoid, with the $\sigma^2$ variant, against the same kind
  of null.
\item \emph{Write back} onto the events, and, with \emph{write\_group}, set \code{group} via
  \code{write_group_columns} to the family key: the number, \texttt{-1} for none,
  \texttt{<n>\textasciitilde noise\_like} for a flagged family,
  \texttt{<n>\textasciitilde untested} for a family no null was drawn for (always so under
  \texttt{ward}); with an upstream \code{group}, \code{<upstream>/<key>}, the original kept in
  \code{group_upstream}.
\end{enumerate}

\entryfield{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Units.} Not unit-aware and need not be: every window is z-normalised. Window
  lengths are in seconds, turned into samples with $f_s$.
\item \emph{The signal.} Windows are cut from the signal the chain hands this block (there
  is no waveform side input). If the detector ran on a short-detrended trace, the shapes
  compared are the detrended ones.
\item \emph{Missing values.} Up to \emph{max\_nan\_frac} of a window is interpolated; more,
  and the event is left out. Quiet-stretch null windows must hold no NaN.
\item \emph{Edges.} An event whose window would cross either end of the span is left out:
  windows are never padded.
\item \emph{Event count.} A family needs at least \emph{min\_group} (10) members; a span with
  a handful of events gives no families.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Outputs}
A \texttt{SpanSet} with the spans and upstream columns unchanged, plus \code{family},
\code{family_omit_reason} (\code{""} when placed), \code{family_distance} (to the medoid on
the method's 0--1 scale; NaN off a family) and \code{family_noise_like}; these are
bookkeeping columns, which a later classifier or clustering never reads as features. With
\emph{write\_group}, \code{group} and possibly \code{group_upstream}.

\code{meta} carries each family's size, flags, null kind and coherence (from
\code{family_meta} in \code{Adapters/catalogue_shape_families.py}), \code{omitted} (counts by
reason), \code{n_events}, \code{n_windows}, \code{fraction_placed},
\code{fraction_confident} (events in families that were tested and passed, over all
events; \code{None} under \texttt{ward}), \code{exemplars} and \code{exemplar_events} (each
family's medoid event), \code{window} (length, anchor, offset, fallbacks), \code{notes},
\code{warnings}, \code{rules}, \code{seconds} and a one-line \code{summary}.

\entryfield{Cost}
One window per event. \texttt{hdbscan} builds an $n\times n$ distance matrix over at most
\emph{hdbscan\_sample} (5{,}000) windows; \texttt{kshape} repeats its fit
\emph{kshape\_n\_init} times; \texttt{ward} is refused past 5{,}000. The coherence costs about
1.5--2\,ms per member (\code{COHERENCE_SECONDS_PER_ITEM}). The run's estimate is the
method's own (\code{estimate_for}), which assumes an event density because it cannot see the
events.

\entryfield{Failure modes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Families in noise.} On rec 6's drop chain, phase-randomised copies of the same
  events were placed in families as often as the real ones, though the block itself flagged
  those noise crowds in 18 of 20 draws (\code{docs/prompts/blocks/reports/W2-evaluation.md}).
  Read \code{family_noise_like} and the coherence before the rhythm.
\item \emph{Smooth shapes read as noise.} Against the trend-kept null, a smooth hump or ramp
  that fills its window can fail without being noise; the quiet-stretch null was added for
  this, and is used only where the chain leaves enough quiet signal (the family says which).
\item \emph{The anchor and length are placeholders}, measured only on synthetic shapes:
  span start plus 600\,s kept two shapes apart in 18 of 18 cases; Event shape's onset as
  anchor did much worse (adjusted Rand 0.13--0.27 against 1.00 on the template's synthetic
  recording), and the automatic length only 5 of 18 (adapter docstring).
\item \emph{Overlapping windows.} Events closer than a window share samples; nothing is
  refused, a warning is raised.
\item \emph{Ward} families are never tested: their keys are always
  \texttt{\textasciitilde untested}.
\end{itemize}

\entryfield{Validation}
\code{tests/test_shape_families.py}: two clear-cut synthetic shapes give two confident
families; phase-randomised noise is mostly in no family; families are numbered by size; the
exemplar is the medoid; a mixed family scores less coherent than a pure one; the same seed
gives the same families. \code{tests/test_adapter_event_families.py}: cut events of pure
coloured noise (random walk and AR(1), 200 and 1{,}000 events) leave no unmarked Intervals
key at the defaults. \code{tests/test_template_event_families.py}: the template recovers two
planted families and Intervals reports each one's rhythm within 10\,\%. On real recordings
(\code{W2-evaluation.md}) the template beat its null on 1 of the 5 spans that held events
(rec 542: 87\,\% of 163 events in two families, no null draw placed any) and found no family
on 24\,h M2\_aug spans; hence ``experimental''.
